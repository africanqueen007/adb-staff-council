# The Fine Print Club, Episode 1: "Welcome to the Club"

**Pilot script draft, about 6 minutes.** Shooting format: phone camera, one location, as few takes as possible.

Every fact in this script about the Staff Association comes from ADB's own page.[^sa] Anything in **[square brackets]** is a placeholder for you to fill in. I have not invented details I cannot verify, such as the number of Council members or how staff reach you.

**Cast:**

- **THE CHAIR** (you)
- **OFF-CAMERA COLLEAGUE** (heard, never seen; also holds the camera)
- **COLLEAGUES** (real staff, filmed with consent)
- **MISSION COLLEAGUE** (a resident mission colleague, on a video call or selfie video)

---

## Cold Open: The Summons (0:00–0:40)

*Interior, Staff Council office. The phone camera is at eye level. The frame is empty. An office chair rolls in from the side. THE CHAIR is on it, holding a phone, and stops dead center.*

**THE CHAIR** *(quietly, to camera)*: So. Someone sent me this.

*(Reads from the phone.)*

**THE CHAIR:** "Hi. I've worked at ADB for [number] years. What is the Staff Council, and why do I keep getting emails from it?"

*(Long beat. Looks at the camera.)*

**THE CHAIR:** [Number] years.

*(Puts the phone down. Leans in.)*

**THE CHAIR:** Pull up a chair.

## Title Sting (0:40–0:50)

*A thick binder lands on the desk. Four colored tabs: orange, yellow, green, blue. Text on screen: **THE FINE PRINT CLUB**.*

## 🟧 Orange Tab: "You're Already In" (0:50–1:50)

*THE CHAIR opens the binder to the orange tab.*

**THE CHAIR:** Here's what nobody tells you on your first day. From the day you join ADB, you are automatically part of the ADB Staff Association.

*(Beat.)*

**THE CHAIR:** No sign-up. You've been a member this whole time.

**OFF-CAMERA COLLEAGUE:** Since when?

**THE CHAIR:** Since your first day. The Association itself has been around since 1978.

**OFF-CAMERA COLLEAGUE:** Were you there in 1978?

**THE CHAIR** *(to camera, unblinking)*: Still with me?

## 🟨 Yellow Tab: "Who We Are" (1:50–2:50)

*Yellow tab.*

**THE CHAIR:** The Staff Association is run by an executive body. That's the Staff Council. That's us. [Number] colleagues from [departments / resident missions; fill in].

*(Text on screen: names and photos of Council members, if they agree.)*

**THE CHAIR:** We're backed by committees and working groups. And our job, in one sentence: we stand up for the rights, interests, and welfare of staff, by talking with Management. Continuously.

**OFF-CAMERA COLLEAGUE:** Continuously?

**THE CHAIR:** *(Slowly turns the binder to show how thick it is.)*

## Field Trip: "Does Anyone Know What We Do?" (2:50–4:20)

*Exterior shots around the building: corridor, cafeteria, lobby. THE CHAIR walks up to colleagues with the phone.*

**THE CHAIR:** Quick question. What does the Staff Council do?

*(Use real, unscripted answers from colleagues who agree to be filmed. Keep the funniest honest ones. Do not stage fake answers.)*

**OFF-CAMERA COLLEAGUE** *(whispering)*: Should we tell them it's you?

**THE CHAIR** *(whispering back)*: Not yet.

*Cut to MISSION MAIL: a 10-second selfie video from a resident mission colleague.*

**MISSION COLLEAGUE:** Hi from [resident mission]. [Their real question about the Staff Council.]

**THE CHAIR** *(to camera)*: Great question. Hold that thought.

## 🟩 Green Tab: The Recipe (4:20–5:10)

*Reading glasses on. The pace picks up. Each line appears on screen as it is spoken.*

**THE CHAIR:** The Staff Council works on six things:

1. Compensation and benefits
2. Talent and performance management
3. Equity and inclusion
4. Workplace culture and justice
5. Staff welfare and connection
6. Policy and governance

**THE CHAIR:** How to reach us: [inbox / intranet page].
**THE CHAIR:** How to send a question for this show: [link or address].
**THE CHAIR:** *(to the MISSION COLLEAGUE)* Your answer: [the real answer].

## 🟦 Blue Tab: The Promise (5:10–5:40)

*Glasses still on. Tone drops; this part is sincere.*

**THE CHAIR:** Here's how this series works. Every time a policy changes, I'll tell you three things. What changed. What you need to do. And what the Council said about it. If we pushed back, I'll tell you. If it's still being discussed, I'll tell you that too.

## Button (5:40–6:00)

**THE CHAIR:** So that's the club. You were already in it.

*(Takes off glasses.)*

**THE CHAIR:** But what do I know. I only read the whole thing.

*(Rolls out of frame on the chair.)*

*End card, deadpan text:* **Update: [the colleague from the cold open] has now opened one Staff Council email.** *(Only use this if they agree. Otherwise, write a different true update from the shoot.)*

*Final card: ADB Staff Association logo. "Send us your question. It might be next episode's Summons." [Link.]*
